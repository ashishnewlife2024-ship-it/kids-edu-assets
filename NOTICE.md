# Attribution and licensing

This repository holds image assets used by the PlayNest app.

## `animals/` — 10 photographs

**Source:** Wikimedia Commons — https://commons.wikimedia.org

**Status: attribution INCOMPLETE. This notice is not yet sufficient.**

These photographs were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and renamed. The
per-file author, the source page and the specific licence each file carries
were **not recorded at the time they were harvested**, and are not recoverable
from the files themselves (they carry no EXIF author or rights metadata).

This matters, and the reason is worth stating plainly:

- **Wikimedia Commons is a host, not an author.** "Wikimedia Commons
  contributors" does not name anybody, and Commons does not issue a licence.
- **Commons has no single licence.** Individual files are CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0,
  or public domain, and the conditions differ. CC BY and CC BY-SA both require
  naming the author and identifying the licence for *each* work.

Until each file's author, source page and licence are restored, this repository
does not meet those conditions. It is published here rather than quietly, so the
gap is visible to anyone who looks.

**Being done about it,** in order of preference:

1. Trace each of the 10 files back to its Commons page and record the author,
   the licence and a link to the original, per file, in this notice.
2. Failing that, replace them with images whose provenance is recorded at the
   point of use.

If you are the author of one of these photographs and want attribution
corrected or the image removed, please open an issue on this repository and it
will be actioned.
